# External Assets & Third-Party Brand Attribution

This file records third-party assets (logos, icons, brand marks, sound files,
etc.) bundled in this repository, together with the licence or usage grant that
permits their inclusion.

> **Rule (see AGENTS.md §4 / docs/plugins.rst):** A third-party brand or
> trademark logo may be committed **only** if its owner's licence, trademark
> policy, or press kit **explicitly permits** using the mark to refer to that
> software/service. When permitted, the asset must be:
> 1. used **unmodified** (no alteration of colours, shape, proportions);
> 2. used **only to refer to the original project** — never as SyntH's own
>    branding, and never implying endorsement;
> 3. **attributed here**, with a link to the governing licence/policy.
>
> If the licence does not grant this, ship an original non-branded glyph
> instead (see `scripts/generate_component_icons.py`) or rely on the SyntH
> logo fallback.

## AzuraCast

- **Asset(s):** AzuraCast logo, used as the icon for the `radio_host` plugin's
  AzuraCast integration.
- **Owner:** AzuraCast (Buster "Silver Eagle" Neece and contributors).
- **Software licence:** AGPL-3.0 —
  <https://github.com/AzuraCast/AzuraCast/blob/main/LICENSE.md>
- **Brand grant:** The official AzuraCast Press Kit explicitly welcomes related
  code projects to "utilize our branding as needed to refer to our software"
  and states you are "free to use our branding to refer to AzuraCast itself."
  Press Kit + guidelines: <https://www.azuracast.com/docs/press-kit>
- **Guideline compliance:** The logo is used **unmodified**, solely to identify
  the AzuraCast integration inside SyntH. SyntH does not present itself as
  AzuraCast, does not call any fork "AzuraCast", and does not use the mark as
  its own logo.
